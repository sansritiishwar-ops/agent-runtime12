# Blog Formatting for Fashion Publication

Use this skill when you have completed research and gathered content for a fashion blog post, and now need to format it into a polished, publication-ready document. This skill ensures your output meets professional fashion publishing standards with industry-specific formatting and style.

## When to Use This Skill

**Use this skill when:**
- You have researched fashion content and need to format it as a blog post
- You are creating trend reports, style guides, or seasonal fashion analysis
- You need to format outfit inspiration, styling tips, or how-to-wear content
- You are writing designer spotlights, brand features, or fashion event coverage
- You are creating shopping guides or product roundups
- You want to ensure the fashion blog post meets professional structure and readability standards
- You are finalizing fashion content for immediate publishing

**Do NOT use this skill when:**
- You are still in the research or information-gathering phase
- You are creating technical documentation or academic papers (different formatting standards apply)
- You are writing social media captions or short-form content (different format)
- You need to create images or edit photos (this skill focuses on text formatting)
- You are writing non-fashion content (use general blog formatting instead)

## Fashion Content Types and Their Structures

### Trend Reports
- **Opening**: Identify the trend and its relevance to current season
- **Analysis**: Break down the trend (colors, silhouettes, styling elements)
- **Runway/Street Style Examples**: Reference where the trend is appearing
- **How to Wear**: Practical styling advice for different occasions
- **Shopping**: Product recommendations at various price points

### Style Guides and How-Tos
- **Problem/Occasion**: What styling challenge or event this addresses
- **The Formula**: Step-by-step outfit building or styling approach
- **Visual Breakdown**: Describe each piece and why it works
- **Variations**: How to adapt the look for different body types, budgets, or seasons
- **Styling Tips**: Detailed advice on proportions, accessories, fit

### Outfit Inspiration Posts
- **The Look**: Overall vibe and occasion
- **Outfit Breakdown**: Each piece with details (brand, price, alternatives)
- **Styling Notes**: Why pieces work together, styling tricks used
- **Shop the Look**: Direct links or similar product suggestions
- **Recreate It**: How readers can achieve a similar look

### Designer/Brand Spotlights
- **Background**: Designer/brand story and aesthetic
- **Signature Elements**: What they're known for
- **Current Collection/Season**: Highlights and standout pieces
- **Price Point & Availability**: Where to buy and typical pricing
- **Who It's For**: Target audience and styling personality

### Shopping Guides and Roundups
- **Category Introduction**: What you're shopping for and why now
- **Selection Criteria**: What makes these picks noteworthy
- **Product Features**: Organized by subcategory, price, or style
- **Styling Suggestions**: How to wear each featured item
- **Quick Picks Summary**: Top 3-5 recommendations for easy scanning

## Structure Requirements

### Title and Heading Hierarchy
- **Main Title (H1)**: One compelling, clear H1 title that captures the fashion topic and includes relevant style keywords
  - Examples: "10 Spring Trends to Wear Now", "How to Style Wide-Leg Jeans for Every Occasion", "Minimalist Wardrobe Essentials for 2024"
- **Section Headings (H2)**: Use H2 for major sections (typically 3-6 main sections in fashion posts)
  - Examples: "The Trend", "How to Wear It", "Shop the Look", "Styling Tips", "Our Picks"
- **Subsection Headings (H3)**: Use H3 for subsections, particularly in outfit breakdowns or product categories
  - Examples: "For Day", "For Night", "Under $100", "Investment Pieces"
- **Keep hierarchy simple**: Avoid H4 and below; reorganize content instead

### Introduction Pattern for Fashion Posts
- **Hook**: Start with a trend observation, style question, or seasonal reference that captures attention
  - Example: "Wide-leg jeans are everywhere this season, but styling them can feel intimidating."
- **Context**: Establish why this topic matters now (trending, seasonal, timely, solves a problem)
- **Personal perspective**: Brief mention of your take or experience with the trend/style
- **Preview**: Indicate what the post will cover without giving everything away
- **Length**: Keep introductions to 2-3 paragraphs (100-200 words) for fashion content

### Body Structure for Fashion Content
- **Logical flow**: Organize sections to build from concept to practical application
  - Typical flow: Trend/concept → Why it matters → How to wear it → Product recommendations
- **Visual-first thinking**: Structure content around where images will appear
- **Paragraph length**: Keep paragraphs to 2-4 sentences (40-80 words) for high visual content
- **Product integration**: Naturally weave product mentions and links throughout relevant sections
- **Styling details matter**: Include specific details (fabric, fit, proportion, color combinations)
- **Transitions**: Use fashion-specific transitions ("To complete the look...", "For a more casual take...", "Style it with...")

### Conclusion Pattern for Fashion Posts
- **Style summary**: Recap the key styling takeaway or trend insight
- **Encouragement**: Inspire readers to try the look or trend with confidence
- **Engagement CTA**: Ask about their style preferences, favorite pieces, or how they'd wear it
- **Next steps**: Suggest related content, shopping actions, or styling experiments
- **Length**: Keep conclusions concise (2-3 paragraphs, 80-120 words)

## Fashion-Specific Readability

### Fashion Voice and Tone
- **Aspirational yet accessible**: Inspire without intimidating; make high fashion feel attainable
- **Confident authority**: Write with styling expertise and conviction ("This works because..." not "This might work...")
- **Personal and conversational**: Use first person ("I love...", "My go-to...") and second person ("You can...", "Your best bet...")
- **Enthusiastic but not breathless**: Express genuine excitement without excessive exclamation points
- **Descriptive and specific**: Use precise fashion language ("oversized blazer" not just "jacket"; "barrel-leg jeans" not just "jeans")

### Fashion Terminology Guidelines
- **Current terms**: Use on-trend fashion language ("quiet luxury" not "understated elegance" if that's the current phrase)
- **Explain when needed**: Define niche terms on first use ("dopamine dressing (wearing bright, joy-sparking colors)")
- **Avoid dated language**: Skip terms that feel outdated ("fashionista", "on fleek")
- **Brand names**: Use correct brand capitalization and spellings (Hermès, Saint Laurent, COS)
- **Style categories**: Use recognized category terms (streetwear, coastal grandmother, dark academia)

### Fashion Sentence Construction
- **Vary sentence length**: Mix short style statements ("The result? Effortless." ) with medium descriptive sentences
- **Active voice for styling**: "Pair the blazer with..." not "The blazer can be paired with..."
- **Sensory language**: Describe textures, drape, fit, and visual impact
- **Concrete style advice**: Replace vague statements with specific guidance
  - Vague: "Style it nicely"
  - Specific: "Tuck in the front and leave the back loose for a relaxed, balanced silhouette"

## Visual Content Strategy for Fashion

### Image Placement and Density
- **High visual frequency**: Include image placeholders every 150-250 words
- **Lead image**: Always suggest a hero image after the title/introduction
- **Strategic placement**: Place images where they illustrate the point being discussed
- **Visual variety**: Mix full outfit shots, detail close-ups, styling variations, and product features

## Fashion SEO Best Practices

### Fashion Keywords
- **Seasonal terms**: Include current season/year ("Fall 2024 trends", "Spring fashion")
- **Trend names**: Use exact trend terminology people search ("coastal grandmother", "quiet luxury")
- **Style categories**: Target specific niches ("minimalist fashion", "sustainable style", "petite styling")
- **Product-specific**: Optimize for how-to searches ("how to style mom jeans", "best winter coats under $200")
- **Designer/brand names**: Include recognizable names naturally
- **Occasion-based**: Include event/occasion keywords ("wedding guest outfit", "work-from-home style")

### Fashion Meta Description
- **Include key trend/product**: Mention the specific fashion topic
- **Add value proposition**: What they'll learn or get ("discover how to...", "shop the best...")
- **Seasonal relevance**: Reference current season if applicable
- **150-160 characters**: Stay within character limit
- **Example**: "Discover the top 10 Fall 2024 fashion trends and learn how to style each one for your wardrobe. Shop our curated picks."

### Fashion Linking Strategy
- **Product affiliate links**: Include shopping links naturally within styling descriptions
  - Natural: "I love [this oversized blazer from Everlane](link) for its structured shoulders"
  - Unnatural: "Click here to shop"
- **Internal links**: Link to related style guides, similar trends, or complementary content (2-4 per post)
  - "If you loved this, check out our guide to [styling ankle boots](link)"
- **External sources**: Link to designer collections, trend reports, or authoritative fashion sources (1-2 per post)
- **Strategic placement**: Place product links at natural decision points

### URL-Friendly Title for Fashion
- **Include main keyword**: Primary trend or product term
- **Keep concise**: 3-6 words ideal
- **Use hyphens**: Separate words with hyphens
- **Skip articles**: Remove "the", "a", "an" when possible
- **Examples**: 
  - "How to Style Wide-Leg Jeans" → `style-wide-leg-jeans`
  - "Best White Sneakers Under $150" → `best-white-sneakers-under-150`

## Fashion-Specific Content Elements

### Product Attribution and Shopping Links
**When featuring specific products:**
- **Brand and product name**: "Toteme Oversized Wool Blazer"
- **Price point**: Include current price or price range
- **Availability note**: "Available at Net-a-Porter" or "Currently sold out, similar options:"
- **Alternatives**: Suggest similar options at different price points
- **Format**: Use clean, readable attribution
  ```markdown
  **The Blazer**: Toteme Oversized Wool Blazer, $890 at Net-a-Porter [link]
  **Budget-Friendly**: Mango Structured Wool Blazer, $149 [link]
  ```

### Styling Tips Sections
Format practical styling advice clearly:
- **Use bullet points** for quick styling tips
- **Be specific** about proportions, fit, and techniques
- **Include "why"** - explain the styling principle

**Example:**
```markdown
### Styling Tips

- **Balance proportions**: Wide-leg jeans work best with fitted or cropped tops to define your waistline
- **Hem length matters**: Aim for the hem to hit just above your ankle bone or graze the floor in heels
- **The half-tuck**: Tuck just the front of your shirt to create shape while keeping a relaxed vibe
- **Shoe choices**: Pointed-toe heels elongate the leg; platform sneakers add edge while maintaining the wide silhouette
```

### Size, Fit, and Fabric Information
Include practical details when relevant:
- **Fit notes**: "Runs large, size down" or "True to size"
- **Model sizing**: "Model is 5'7" wearing size S"
- **Fabric feel**: "Soft cashmere blend" or "Structured cotton twill"
- **Care instructions**: "Machine washable" or "Dry clean only" for investment pieces

### Seasonal and Trend Timeliness
- **Date appropriately**: Reference current season/year to establish timeliness
- **Trend context**: Explain where a trend came from ("seen on runways", "street style favorite")
- **Longevity note**: Indicate if it's a long-term investment or seasonal fun piece
- **Transition tips**: Show how pieces work across seasons

## Fashion Post Length Guidelines

### Target Word Counts by Post Type
- **Quick styling tip or single outfit**: 500-800 words
- **How-to style guide**: 800-1,200 words
- **Trend report**: 1,000-1,500 words
- **Comprehensive shopping guide**: 1,200-1,800 words
- **Designer feature or deep dive**: 1,500-2,000+ words

### Section Pacing for Fashion
- **Introduction**: 10-15% (establish trend/topic relevance)
- **Main content**: 60-70% (styling advice, product features, trend analysis)
- **Shopping/product section**: 15-20% (product recommendations and links)
- **Conclusion**: 5-10% (style summary and CTA)

## Professional Polish for Fashion Content

### Fashion Fact-Checking
- **Designer names and brands**: Verify correct spelling and capitalization
- **Runway references**: Confirm collection season and year
- **Product prices**: Ensure pricing is current at time of publishing
- **Trend names**: Use accurate, recognized terminology
- **Product availability**: Verify items are currently in stock or note if sold out

### Fashion Grammar and Style
- **Brand name consistency**: Maintain exact brand capitalization throughout
- **Seasonal capitalization**: "Spring 2024" (capitalize season when paired with year)
- **Price formatting**: Use $ symbol, no decimals for whole numbers ($150 not $150.00)
- **Size formatting**: Use consistent size notation (XS, S, M, L, XL or 0, 2, 4, 6, 8)

### Fashion Call-to-Action Examples
- **Engagement**: "Which trend are you most excited to try? Tell us in the comments!"
- **Shopping**: "Shop our full curated edit of [trend] pieces below."
- **Sharing**: "Save this post for your next shopping trip!"
- **Community**: "Tag us in your styled looks—we love seeing how you wear it!"
- **Content**: "Subscribe for weekly style guides and trend reports delivered to your inbox."
